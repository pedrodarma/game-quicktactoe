How to play?

The QuickTacToe is a quick version of the classic Tic Tac Toe game.

    1 - Play against a friend in real-time.
    2 - Each player has a limited time to make their move.
    3 - First to get 3 in a row wins!
    4 - Can you beat your opponent before time runs out?
