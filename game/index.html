How to play?

The QuickTacToe is a quick version of the classic Tic Tac Toe game.

1 - The game is played on a 3x3 grid. You are X, your opponent is O.

2 - The game starts automatically as soon as both players are connected.

3 - Players take turns putting their marks in empty squares. The first player to get 3 of her marks in a row (up, down, across, or diagonally) is the winner.

4 - When all 9 squares are full, the game is over.

5 - If no player has 3 marks in a row, the game ends in a tie.

6 - If the timer runs out before a player makes a move, they forfeit the game.
